Online counselling for Iranians in Yerevan: common reasons people reach out

Most people who contact us from Yerevan are dealing with one of these. For each one we have therapists who work with it all the time.

Stress and anxiety after moving

Residence, finding work in your field, the cost of living in Yerevan and worrying about family in Iran. When these pile up, they take your sleep and focus too.

Read: Anxiety After Immigration: Why It Happens and What Helps

Loneliness and missing family

You can feel alone even inside a big Iranian community. Homesickness can start to look like depression, and it helps to talk early, not once you've run out of energy.

Read: How to Deal With Homesickness Abroad When You Miss Your Family

Couples and marriage counselling

Moving abroad shifts roles in a relationship: one partner finds work first, the other wants to go back. Couples therapy in Farsi helps keep these from turning into fights.

Read: Farsi Speaking Marriage Counselor: Online Couples Therapy for Iranians Abroad

Family, children and two languages

A child who speaks another language at school in Yerevan and Farsi at home can feel stuck between two worlds. Our child and adolescent therapists work with parents too.

Read: Raising Bilingual Children in an Iranian Family Abroad: Language, Identity and Common Worries

Uncertainty and living in limbo

Waiting for residence, for the next visa, for news from home. Living in limbo is exhausting, and you can learn to cope with it.

Read: Anxiety Before Moving Abroad: When You Haven't Left Yet but Can't Settle
